Battleship

You versus the computer — sink all five enemy ships first.

How to play

  1. Place your fleet. Pick a ship, press R to switch between horizontal and vertical, then click a square on Your Fleet to drop it. Ships can't overlap or hang off the edge. Place Randomly does it all for you, and Reset Placement clears the board so you can start the layout over.
  2. Start the game. The button unlocks once all five ships are down. The computer hides its own fleet in Enemy Waters and you shoot first.
  3. Fire. Click a square on Enemy Waters. Red is a hit, white is a miss, and you're told which ship you struck or sank. The opponent then takes its shot at your fleet. The Message log shows the last few moves — scroll up inside it to read every shot of the game so far.
  4. Win. Sink all five enemy ships before it sinks yours. The banner above the boards always says whose turn it is and what just happened.
